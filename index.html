What is Ayurved?

The word 'Ayurved' is the combination of two words Ayu(life) and Veda (knowledge). It is one of the most ancient medical sciences of the world. It roots in Atharva-Veda, the oldest recorded compendium of wisdom on the earth (6000 B.C.)

Ayurved conceives & describes the basic and applied aspects of life process, health, disease and its management with prevention in terms of its own principles and approaches body.
